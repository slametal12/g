Tracey D. Brown

Tracey D. Brown was the CEO of the American Diabetes Association until October 6, 2021, when she left to become President of Retail Products and Chief Customer Officer at Walgreens. She is their first CEO to have been diagnosed with type 2 diabetes. Before becoming CEO in 2018, she was with Sam’s Club as a senior vice president, CEO and managing director of RAPP Dallas and COO for the direct marketing agency Direct Impact.





== Education ==

Brown trained as a chemical engineer and has an MBA from Columbia University.
